What people

How long does it take to publish a sefer?

It depends on the length of the manuscript and the services you need. A short kuntress can be ready in a few weeks; a full-length sefer that needs typing, editing, typesetting and a cover usually runs a few months. We set a realistic schedule with you at the start and keep to it.

What file formats do you accept for typing and editing?

Anything you have. Handwritten manuscripts, scans, photographs of pages, printed seforim, Word files, PDFs — our typists are used to working from difficult handwriting and poor-quality scans.

Can you work from handwritten manuscripts?

Yes — this is one of our specialties. Our typists are proficient even with difficult handwriting, and every page is checked by a talmid chochom who understands the content, not just the letters.

Which typesetting style should I choose for my sefer?

Single column suits derashos, sichos and most chiddushim. Double column suits halachic works and longer texts. Multi-text is for a sefer printed alongside a commentary or a mekor. We will advise you based on the content of your sefer.

Do you design the cover as well?

Yes. We work with the finest graphic artists who specialize in seforim, so the cover, the shaar blatt and the spine all match the character of the sefer inside.

Do you handle printing and binding?

We provide full printing services with top-notch results for both soft and hard cover, in any quantity — from a small kuntress for a bris to a full print run.
